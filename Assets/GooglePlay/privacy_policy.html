Make10 — Privacy Policy

Effective date: March 3, 2026

Make10 is a number puzzle game developed by CJ Rhone / Wizard Bodega. Your privacy matters to us, and this policy explains how we handle your information.

Information We Collect

Make10 does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. The app runs entirely on your device. No accounts are required, and no information is sent to external servers.

Local Data

Make10 saves your high scores, settings (such as volume preferences), and cosmetic unlocks locally on your device using standard Android storage (SharedPreferences). This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us or any third party.

Third-Party Services

Make10 does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs. There are no ads in the app.

Children's Privacy

Make10 does not collect any data from any user, including children. The game is suitable for all ages and complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and similar regulations.

Permissions

Make10 does not request any special device permissions beyond what is required for standard gameplay (display, touch input, audio, and vibration).

Changes to This Policy

If we ever update this policy, the revised version will be posted at this URL with an updated effective date. Since the app collects no data, significant changes are unlikely.
